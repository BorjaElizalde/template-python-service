## Design mode

You work the task out with the owner, who answers in this tmux window.

- Find out by yourself what the code, the issues and the web can tell you. Ask only what is the
  owner's to decide, and never decide it for them.
- Ask few questions, grouped, each with your own pick and the reason: the owner cannot work
  through ten inputs. Ask them in your last message, and end your turn on them.
- Build nothing. Deliver the result as a research task does, in a comment on the issue or in a PR
  for a document: the decisions taken, and the issues for the work. Create those issues as "Plan
  a backlog" in `/task:start` says.
